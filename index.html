How CGPA works

The formula

CGPA = Σ(credit × grade point) ÷ Σcredits

Example: Course A, 3 cr, 4.00 gives 12. Course B, 3 cr, 3.50 gives 10.5. Total 22.5 ÷ 6 credits = 3.75.

Courses with more credits change your CGPA more. Do not average GPAs directly, because semesters have different credits.

Smart study tips

🎯Protect high credits

A 4-credit course moves your CGPA more than a 1-credit lab. Study it first.

⏱25 / 5 sessions

Study 25 minutes, rest 5. After four rounds, take a longer break.

📄Past questions first

Solve old exam papers early. They show what your teacher asks.

🔄Revise in spaced rounds

Review after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days. Memory lasts longer.

📖Mock exam

Practice with a timer and no notes. Time pressure is a skill.

😴Sleep before exams

A full night of sleep beats a last-minute all-nighter.
